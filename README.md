## A little about me

I like turning rough ideas into clear, dependable tools. My approach is equal parts curiosity and craft: learn the shape of a problem, make a small useful version, then keep improving it. I’m especially drawn to clean interfaces, thoughtful automation, and the details that make software feel good to use.

Away from the keyboard, I’m usually collecting ideas for the next small thing worth making. 🌱
